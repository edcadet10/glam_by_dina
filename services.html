Frequently Asked Questions

How do we get started working together?

The process begins with a discovery call to understand your needs and objectives. From there, I'll prepare a custom proposal with timeline and pricing details. Once approved, we'll kickoff with a detailed briefing session before diving into the creative work.

What is your typical turnaround time?

Turnaround times vary based on project complexity and current workload. Standard projects typically take 1-2 weeks from approval to delivery, while more complex campaigns may require 3-4 weeks. Rush options are available for time-sensitive projects at an additional fee.

Do you offer custom packages?

Absolutely! While the packages shown represent common configurations, I'm happy to create a tailored solution specific to your needs and budget. Custom packages are developed based on your unique requirements, timeline, and desired outcomes.

What does the revision process look like?

All projects include two rounds of revisions to ensure your complete satisfaction. The revision process is collaborative, with clear feedback incorporated at each stage. Additional revision rounds can be arranged for an extra fee if needed.

How do you price your services?

Pricing is determined by several factors including project scope, complexity, timeline, usage rights, and distribution needs. I provide transparent pricing with no hidden fees, and all proposals clearly outline exactly what's included in the investment.

What rights do I have to the content?

Standard packages include a limited license for specific uses outlined in our agreement. Premium and Enterprise packages include more extensive usage rights. Full content ownership transfers are available for an additional fee. All licensing terms are clearly defined before project commencement.
